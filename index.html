What this show is

Think of it like a voice note from a friend who happens to know a lot about money.

Every week I sit down — usually with a coffee that went cold — and share what's actually happening in my life as a new mom and someone who works in finance. Some episodes are reflective. Some are practical. Some are just me processing something out loud.

Occasionally I bring in a guest: someone whose perspective I think you'll love. But mostly it's just me, being honest about the journey.

My story

Hi, I'm Jessica. So glad you're here.

I started this podcast because I couldn't find what I was looking for: something honest, warm, and specifically for women navigating a career in finance while figuring out motherhood for the very first time.

I spent over a decade working in investment management before having my daughter, Lily. And I had absolutely no idea how much that would change — and not change — how I showed up at work, at home, and for myself.

So I started talking about it. I hope something here helps you feel a little more seen.
