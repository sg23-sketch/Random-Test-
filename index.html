The Show

Finance didn't pause for your baby. We get it.

The Portfolio & Pacifier is the podcast that no one made when we needed it most — so we made it ourselves. Each week we sit down with women who are crushing it in finance while figuring out sleep schedules, maternity leave politics, pumping at work, and everything in between.

Whether you're an analyst on the trading floor, an advisor building your book, or an aspiring CFO with a newborn on your hip — this is your community.

  • Negotiating maternity leave in male-dominated firms
  • Returning to work: identity, confidence & childcare math
  • Building wealth while paying for diapers
  • Setting financial goals as a new family
  • Mental health & the pressure of "having it all"

Your Host

Jessica Morgan

Former portfolio manager turned podcast host, Jessica spent a decade on the buy-side before having her daughter, Lily. She started The Portfolio & Pacifier after discovering how little support existed for women in finance who were also becoming mothers for the first time.

Jessica holds a CFA designation and an MBA from Wharton. She now lives in New York City with her husband, her daughter, and one very judgy cat.
